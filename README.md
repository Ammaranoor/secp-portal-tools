## Overview
The **SECP Portal** simplifies corporate registration, company name search verification, and tax calculations for business owners in Pakistan.

Direct Portal: **[https://secpportal.com.pk/](https://secpportal.com.pk/)**

## Official Features & Capabilities:
* **Company Name Availability Search**: Rapidly verify company name availability against SECP databases.
* **Corporate Tax & FBR Calculators**: Real-time tax computation for private limited and single-member companies.
* **Filing & Compliance Assistance**: Detailed registration guidance for digital incorporation.
